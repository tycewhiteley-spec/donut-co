01 — Trading

Donut Trading

A donut shop that treats every dozen like a handoff, not a transaction.

We fry small batches through the morning so the case is never stale. Dough is proofed in-house, glazes are made from scratch, and the menu changes with the market — seasonal fruit, retail favorites, and one limited run every week.

We trade with the people who come through the door: wholesale to cafés, catering for offices and weddings, and a standing tray for regulars who know exactly what they came for.

  • Fresh, small-batch daily
  • Wholesale & café supply
  • Events & catering

02 — Consulting

Donut Consulting

Fractional operating help for businesses that are past the idea and into the mess.

Strategy on a slide deck is cheap. We do the unglamorous work: pricing that holds up, operations that don't depend on one person, and a pipeline you can actually forecast.

We come in for a sprint or stay on for a season — bookkeeping hygiene, hiring the right first manager, or untangling a process that scaled by accident.

  • Fractional COO / operations
  • Pricing & unit economics
  • Systems, hiring & process

03 — Vehicles

Donut Vehicles

Classic cars, converted to electric. A restomod shop built for the next hundred years.

We take beloved classics — the ones with patina and a story — and swap the drivetrain for modern electric. The original character stays; the leaks, fumes, and starter anxiety go away.

Every build is a full conversion: motor and battery engineering, brake and suspension upgrades to match the new weight, and a cockpit that keeps the original spirit with modern reliability.

  • Full EV conversion, engineered
  • Upgraded brakes & suspension
  • Preserved character, modern internals
